03 · Protocol and layout probes

Radix-4 has a real bind win—and a nontrivial PCS bill

The sound prototype is retained locally; production integration is parked.

Rejected construction

Degree-3d corner interpolation plus ordinary MLE binding/opening is inconsistent. For U(X,Y)=XY, cubic digit interpolation differs from x(Z)y(Z) away from digit nodes. Two ordinary round polynomials sharing one challenge remains forbidden.

Sound shape exercised

  • One four-point polynomial q(Z), one Fiat–Shamir challenge, direct quaternary Lagrange bind.
  • Dense degree-2 relation G=U·V: degree ≤6=3d; four-corner claim identity; terminal relation match.
  • Tamper polynomial cZ(Z−1)(Z−2)(Z−3) preserves every digit node and is rejected at the random point.
  • Metal direct bind-by-two: 1.51–1.98× faster than two binary binds at 2²⁴.
Radix-4 decision
Retain the isolated Metal kernel and soundness tests; kill the scoped production mapping. The measured stage-4 Metal prefix binds committed cycle coordinates, while legal virtual address coordinates require a new state representation and an unpriced protocol rewrite.
Hard blocker: wrong variable order · unchanged-CSR 1.2–2.2 s estimate withdrawn · no active prover change

Address-major

The protocol already supports address-major, but Metal does not: commitments materialize a full strided grid per polynomial and stage 8 falls back to CPU. At 2²², cycle-major finished in 3.52 s / 1.192 MHz; address-major exceeded 240 s at roughly 12 CPU cores and was terminated. CUDA’s sharding prize does not transfer automatically to this unified-memory fast path.

Layout decision

Kill global address-major. Keep targeted block-local AoSoA/transposes as kernel-level experiments only.
